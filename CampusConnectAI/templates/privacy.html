Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This is a plain-language summary of IVY's current data practices. It should be reviewed by a qualified adviser before launch.

1. Who we are

IVY is a peer learning network for students, operated by an individual developer ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact campusconnectaivy@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details you provide: full name, email address, department, academic year, and an optional bio.
  • Profile media: a profile picture and cover photo, if you upload them.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, reactions, direct and group messages, study resources, learning-room messages, and AI assistant conversations.
  • Social data: friend requests, follows, and notifications generated by your activity.
  • Sign-in data: if you use Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. We never receive your Google password.
  • Technical data: your IP address is processed in memory to rate-limit abuse, and we record a "last seen" timestamp for your account.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, including password resets by email.
  • To show your profile and content to other students on the network.
  • To deliver messages, notifications, and real-time study rooms.
  • To provide the AI assistant, whose prompts and replies are sent to our AI provider to generate a response.
  • To keep the service safe by detecting abuse, spam, and excessive requests.

4. Service providers

We share data with the following processors only as needed to run IVY:

  • Supabase - managed Postgres database and file storage.
  • Groq - generates AI assistant replies from the messages you submit to it.
  • Google - provides optional Google Sign-In.
  • Email provider - delivers password reset and account emails.
  • Hosting provider - runs the web application and terminates HTTPS.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Cookies and local storage

IVY uses a single essential session cookie to keep you signed in and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. This cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure on the production site. Your light/dark theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage. IVY does not currently use advertising or third-party analytics cookies, so no non-essential tracking consent is required; if analytics is added later, consent will be requested first.

6. Data retention

Account data and the content you create are kept while your account is active. You can delete your account from your profile, which removes your account and associated content, subject to any records we must keep by law. Password reset and verification tokens expire automatically.

7. Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and never in plain text. Traffic to the production site is encrypted with HTTPS, database access uses parameterised queries, and sensitive headers and rate limits are enforced. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, email campusconnectaivy@gmail.com.

9. Eligibility

IVY is intended for students aged 13 or older. If you believe a younger person has created an account, contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as IVY evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app, and the "last updated" date above will change.
